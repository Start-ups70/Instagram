Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you start.

What is Reels Downloader?

A free tool that saves publicly available Instagram Reels to your device.

How do I download an Instagram Reel?

Copy the Reel link in Instagram, paste it into the box above and tap Download Reel. Choose a quality, then tap Download Video.

Do I need an Instagram account?

No. There is nothing to log in to.

Can I download private Reels?

No. Only Reels from public accounts are supported.

Is the service free?

Yes, Reels Downloader is free to use.

Why isn't my Reel available?

The Reel may be private, deleted or restricted, or the link may be incomplete. Check that it opens when you are logged out of Instagram, then try again.

Why can a download link expire?

Instagram video links are temporary. If a download stops working, paste the Reel link again to get a fresh one.

Does the website require my Instagram password?

Never. We do not ask for passwords or any login details.

Is this website affiliated with Instagram?

No. Reels Downloader is an independent third-party tool and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
